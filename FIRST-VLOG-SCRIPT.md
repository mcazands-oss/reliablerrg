# RELIABLE ROOFING VLOG — Episode 1
## "Why Your Insurance Adjuster Might Be Lowballing You"

**Length:** 4 minutes  
**Gear:** Phone/camera, natural light, standing in front of a roof or damage photo  
**Tone:** Straight-talking, no script-reading (use this as talking points)

---

## OPENING (15 sec)
**[Standing on a roof or with storm damage visible]**

"I'm Scott Karraker, and I've been doing roofing in East Texas for 15 years. Today I want to tell you something about insurance claims that could save you thousands of dollars."

*[Pause. Look at camera]*

"Your insurance adjuster doesn't work for you. They work for the insurance company."

---

## THE PROBLEM (1 min 30 sec)
**[On roof or showing damage]**

"Here's what happens: Storm hits, hail tears up your roof, you call your insurance. An adjuster shows up, spends 20 minutes looking at your roof, and tells you what they'll pay. 

A lot of times, that number is wrong. They miss damage. Or they intentionally lowball you because the faster they close the claim, the better it is for their company.

I've seen it a hundred times. Homeowner gets told their roof damage is $3,000. That's not even close to reality. A full replacement is $12,000 to $18,000."

---

## THE SOLUTION (1 min 30 sec)
**[Back to camera, confident tone]**

"Here's what I do. When a customer has storm damage, I do a *detailed inspection* before the adjuster ever shows up. I photograph everything. I document the damage. I measure the roof.

Then when the adjuster gets there, *I'm standing right there with the homeowner.* I know what to look for. I know what insurance will try to skip over. And I know what needs to be fixed.

That changes the conversation. The adjuster knows I'm not going anywhere, and I know the work better than they do.

Most of the time? We end up with a claim that actually covers the real damage."
