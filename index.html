About Us

15+ Years of East Texas Roofing

Reliable Roofing Restoration & Glass was founded in 2009 by Scott Karraker right here in Gun Barrel City, TX. What started as a one-man operation has grown into one of East Texas's most trusted roofing contractors.

Scott built this company on a simple principle: show up on time, do the work right, and stand behind it. Every project — from a single-family shingle repair to a large commercial metal roof — gets that same personal commitment.
